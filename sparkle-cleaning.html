Sparkle Cleaning

Final-detail cleaning for spaces that need to look ready, polished and professionally presented.

Handover Ready

Presentation Cleaning for the Final Stage

Sparkle cleaning focuses on the fine detail after heavier builders-clean activity has taken place. It is suited to handover plots, show areas, offices, hospitality spaces, retail openings and post-refurbishment presentation.

Detail Areas

  • Internal glazing and frames
  • Touch points, switches and skirtings
  • Sanitaryware and kitchens
  • Joinery, cupboards and shelving

Project Fit

  • Show homes and apartments
  • Office and retail fit-outs
  • Hospitality launches
  • Developer handovers
