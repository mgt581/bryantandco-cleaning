Construction Cleaning

From Progressive Cleaning to Handover

Bryant & Co Cleaning supports construction cleaning requirements from initial builders cleans through to final presentation, snagging revisits and handover readiness. Large projects are individually scoped and quoted.

  • Initial builders clean and waste/dust removal
  • Progressive cleaning during phased works
  • Final builders clean before handover
  • Sparkle clean and presentation detailing
  • Internal glazing and external glazing coordination
  • Floor cleaning, welfare-area cleaning and communal areas
  • Snag/revisit cleaning for issue close-out
  • RAMS-aware planning where required by site
