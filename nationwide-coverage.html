How National Delivery Works

Bryant & Co Cleaning is based in Bournemouth, Dorset. For work outside our core local area, we assess each commercial or construction requirement and coordinate delivery through our own resources and/or vetted, insured regional cleaning partners where appropriate.

  1. You send the requirement: locations, service type, access, dates and site standards.
  2. We check feasibility: location, capacity, skills, insurance and mobilisation.
  3. We quote the project: commercial work is priced by scope, not generic hourly website pricing.
  4. We manage communication: one point of contact for progress, issues and follow-up.

Useful For

  • Construction sites outside Dorset
  • Multi-site commercial cleaning
  • Property portfolio cleaning
  • Regional rollout cleans
  • One-off mobilisation cleans

What We Do Not Claim

We do not claim offices, direct employees or permanent Bryant & Co cleaning teams in every UK town or city. Where regional delivery partners are used, the aim is to keep the client experience coordinated and professionally managed.
