Delivery Model

Managed National Delivery, Honestly Described

We do not claim to have permanent Bryant & Co employed teams in every UK city. For work outside our core Dorset area, we assess each scope and coordinate the right delivery route.

  1. Scope review: send the site list, drawings, cleaning schedule, photos or tender pack.
  2. Delivery planning: we confirm coverage, mobilisation, access, RAMS requirements and project timing.
  3. Managed fulfilment: work is delivered by Bryant & Co teams and/or vetted insured regional partners where suitable.
  4. Communication: you keep one point of contact for updates, issues, sign-off and future requirements.
